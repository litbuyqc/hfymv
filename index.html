02 / Oopbuy shopping guide

From a link
to an informed choice.

Use this four-step process with Oopbuy spreadsheet finds or any other curated product list. Keep each check tied to the exact item and variant you intend to order.

01

Choose a category

Begin with the product type you need and shortlist listings with clear specifications. Decide on your fit, intended use, and budget.

02

Check the exact variant, materials, seller information, stock status, and return terms. A spreadsheet summary may be incomplete or outdated.

Compare listed measurements with a well-fitting item and review available quality-control photos. Ask for missing details before paying.

Calculate the complete budget

Include item cost, domestic delivery, applicable service fees, international shipping, and any taxes or duties.

03 / The comparison desk

What to check
before you commit.

An Oopbuy clothing spreadsheet and an Oopbuy shoes spreadsheet require different comparisons. Use the checks below to avoid treating every listing the same way.

A practical product-link checklist
Product group Check first Clarify before ordering
Shoes Seller size chart, foot-length guidance, upper material, and outsole photos. How measurements were taken and whether the selected size is available.
T-shirts & jerseys Chest width, body length, fabric composition, and print or embroidery details. Whether the chart describes garment measurements or body measurements.
Hoodies & jackets Shoulder width, sleeve length, lining, closure, and room for layering. Fabric weight or insulation details when these affect your intended use.
Pants, shorts & sets Waist, rise, inseam, and separate measurements for each piece of a set. Whether the waist measurement is relaxed or stretched and what the set includes.
Headwear Head circumference, crown depth, brim dimensions, and closure type. The actual adjustment range rather than a general “one size” label.
Jewelry & accessories Stated materials, dimensions, fastening, and care instructions. Any missing composition information, especially if you have material sensitivities.
Underwear Waist measurements, fabric composition, pack quantity, and construction. Hygiene restrictions and whether this product category is eligible for returns.

The price on a row is only one part.

Currency conversion, parcel dimensions, destination, and the chosen delivery method can affect your final payment. Use current quotes and terms rather than an old screenshot.

Item cost + domestic delivery + applicable service fees + international shipping + taxes or duties = estimated total

What is an Oopbuy spreadsheet?

An Oopbuy spreadsheet is generally a curated list of product links used for shopping research. Its contents depend on the person maintaining it; a row does not verify stock, quality, authenticity, or compatibility with an ordering service.

Does this page provide a downloadable spreadsheet?

No. This page provides a category directory and a shopping checklist. Its shopping links lead to CAFANS, including the specified category pages.

How should I check clothing and sneaker sizes?

Compare the listing’s actual measurements with an item that already fits. Check the units and measurement method. For sneakers, compare the seller’s size chart and foot-length guidance rather than relying only on a familiar size label.

What do QC photos show?

Quality-control photos can help you inspect visible details such as color, stitching, labels, and measurements. They cannot by themselves establish authenticity, long-term durability, or an exact fit.

Are the displayed brand images CAFANS product listings?

No. The Nike, Louis Vuitton, Gucci, and Hermès images are editorial references. They do not establish CAFANS availability, seller authorization, authenticity, or a relationship with any brand.

How do I estimate the total shopping cost?

Add item cost, domestic delivery, applicable service fees, international shipping, and any taxes or duties. Confirm the current fee schedule and destination rules before paying.
